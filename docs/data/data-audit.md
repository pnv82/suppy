## Garmin samples

All three original archives were copied byte-for-byte and contain one FIT activity each. ZIP checks and Garmin FIT integrity checks passed. Each decodes as `stand_up_paddleboarding` and has seven laps. No supplied FIT corresponds to the Aug 29 sheet row `24162211256`.

| 24495535896 | Sep 25, 07:53:49 PDT | 898 | 893 | 86.101 / 86.101 | 12 s |

All record rows contain HR, cadence and distance fields. That is field availability, not a guarantee of valid sensor measurement. Enhanced speed is present on the GPS-bearing rows. The files also expose fractional cadence, temperature, cycle-length fields, GPS metadata and unknown/vendor fields; the initial derived track deliberately retains only the basic display channels. A later decoder can inspect richer messages before deciding whether they improve the UI. Do not treat watch temperature as measured ambient weather or cycle length as validated stroke efficiency.

Decoded tracks and field inventories are in `data/derived/`, regenerated by `scripts/inspect_samples.py`. The exports retain irregular timestamps and nullable coordinates; no performance-window analysis was run. Sample summary metrics broadly agree with rounded sheet values. FIT precision and sheet rounding should both be preserved through provenance.

## Existing caution flags to preserve

- Sep 5 and Sep 25: sheet says `Suspect early` HR; exact affected seconds are not supplied. Show a session-level warning, not an invented shaded interval.
- Sep 20: one fall and two interruptions in the sheet; timer duration is about 160.517 s shorter than elapsed duration. Timer events do not establish which stop was a fall or a lifeguard interruption.
- Sep 25: notes describe outbound/return differences and low–medium benchmark quality. This is source analysis, not newly verified leg segmentation.
- Best-window values are `legacy_unspecified` until recalculated with the documented method. Do not relabel the old values as validated continuous efforts.
- No current source proves a particular technique fault, causal wind penalty, or medical cause of symptoms.
